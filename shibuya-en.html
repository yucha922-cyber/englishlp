Frequently Asked Questions

Q. I have never been to a Seitai clinic before. Is that okay?
A. Of course. Most of our first-time guests are new to Seitai. Your Branch Director will walk you through every step, starting with a Consultation & Counseling and AI Posture Analysis.
Q. Does the treatment hurt?
A. No. Our Root Cause Alignment is gentle and is adjusted to each guest's comfort level. Please tell your practitioner anytime if you feel any discomfort.
Q. How often should I visit?
A. It depends on your symptoms, but most guests see meaningful results within 4–8 sessions. Your practitioner will recommend a pace that fits your goals and schedule.
Q. Can I come during pregnancy or right after giving birth?
A. For Postpartum Body Care we recommend starting from around 2 months after delivery. Please ask us in advance if you are currently pregnant.
Q. What should I wear?
A. Comfortable clothing is best. We also have changing wear available free of charge.
Q. Do you accept credit cards?
A. Yes. We accept major credit cards and most cashless payment apps.
